# What is this repository?

This is a fork of the original Mate-Engine repository, modified to add and aim gun at cursor and shoot.

# What is Mate-Engine?

Check out the original repository:
[Mate-Engine](https://github.com/shinyflvre/Mate-Engine)

# How to use

- Run the MateEngineX.exe
- Move your cursor to upper half of the screen to have your avatar takes out gun and aims at it
- Left click to shoot
- Move your cursor to lower half of the screen to have your avatar remove the gun

# Guns
- Cat Gun (automatic). press 1 to switch to this. Hold left click to spray
- M1911 (semi-automatic). press 2 to switch to this (default). Left click up to shoot
